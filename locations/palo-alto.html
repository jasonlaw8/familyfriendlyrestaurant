Popular Palo Alto Neighborhoods for Families

University Avenue

The main downtown strip with diverse restaurants, outdoor patios, and walkable family-friendly dining options.

California Avenue

Charming neighborhood commercial district with local favorites, farmers market, and relaxed atmosphere.

Downtown Palo Alto

Historic downtown area featuring upscale casual dining, cafes, and restaurants with kids menus.

Stanford Area

Near the university campus with casual eateries, coffee shops, and spacious venues perfect for families.
